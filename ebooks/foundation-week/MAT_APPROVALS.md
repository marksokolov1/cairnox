Authority: Mat's messages relayed by the owner in this conversation, 6 November 2026. The owner's subsequent “yeah” approved the polished warm-up with minutes made explicit. ## Replacement programme (source wording)

### Day 1 - Upper Body Strength & off feet conditioning

- 1a) Bench Press - 3x5, 3 second lower, pause, explode up
- 1b) MB Chest pass 3x5
- 2a) Bird Dog Row 3x6
- 2b) Chin Up 3x5-8 (If more than 8 add weight)
- 3a) 1/2 Kneeling DB Press 3x8
- 3b) Banded Facepull 3x12
- 4a) Stability Ball stir the pot 2x8 each way
- 4b) Deadbug 2x8 each way
- Keep the off feet conditioning

### Day 2 - Lower Body Strength & Linear Speed development

- 1a) Trapbar Deadlift 3x5
- 1b) Banded Broad Jump 3x3
- 2a) DB Step up 3x6 each side
- 2b) Single Leg Glute Bridge 3x5 each side
- 3a) Lateral Squat 3x6 each side
- 3b) Heel elevated split squat hold 3x20 seconds
- 4a) Hanging leg raises 2x10
- 4b) Single Arm farmers carry 2x15m
- Speed work: 10m acceleration x5
- Sprint Ladder: Sprint 5m, back pedal 5m, sprint 10m

### Day 3 - Whole Body Power & Lateral Acceleration

- 1a) DB Drop Jump 3x4
- 1b) Med Ball Shot Put 3x3 each side
- 2a) DB Split Jerk 3x4 each side
- 2b) Hamstring switches 3x5 each side
- 3a) TRX Y's & T's 3x6 each way
- 3b) Copenhagen Plank 3x20 seconds
- 3c) Contralateral Plank 3x8 each way
- Lateral Speed work: Shuffle into sprint 3 facing each way
- 5-10-5 x5 reps

## Clarifications received

- Early off season; any age, some training experience recommended. Publication does not turn this into a universal suitability guarantee or invent an age cutoff.
- High-intensity cricket on the same day as training; rest or low-level skills on non-gym days.
- Remove percentages. Select a strength load allowing 1-2 more reps, but no more: RIR.
- Lettered groups are paired/supersets. 30-45 seconds between exercises, 1-2 minutes between sets.
- Day 3's 3A/3B/3C is a three-exercise group with the same rest guidance.
- Bird Dog Row, half-kneeling DB press, split-squat hold, farmers carry and Copenhagen plank are per side; Deadbug also per side. These explicit confirmations override ambiguous original wording.
- Day 1 retains the original off-feet conditioning: 20 seconds ON / 40 seconds OFF, three sets of five, two minutes between sets. Source options: Assault Bike, Ski-Erg or WATT Bike. High effort, not maximal; recovery stationary or light turnover depending on fitness capability.
- Sprint ladder is **2 x 5 complete sequences**; owner relayed Mat's explicit confirmation of this interpretation.
- Shuffle 5m, sprint 10m; three facing each way.
- Speed recovery: walk back or 1 minute between reps; 3-5 minutes between sets.
- Speed can be separate from gym, attached to cricket training or done together, ideally on the same day. No compulsory gym/speed order invented.
- Throws, jumps and jerks: maximal intensity, light-to-moderate weight; velocity the primary goal. RIR applies primarily to strength work, not these movements.

## Approved warm-up copy

Warm-ups may differ depending on the individual. Before each session, complete a 5-10-minute warm-up incorporating general mobility and light-to-moderate activity.

The goal is to feel fully prepared before beginning the Performance Block.
